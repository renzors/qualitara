A normal week for Metro Collision, except that almost no leads converted

From Monday, July 20 at 6:20pm to Monday, July 27 at 6:20pm New York time, Metro Collision's 15 locations logged 81 calls, leads and appointments. That is inside their usual range of 36 to 102. One signal stood out.

Why only "worth a look"? 15 leads is a small sample. One conversion out of 15 is far below usual, but the range for a sample this size is wide (10%–59%). If next week is similar, it becomes unusual.

Leads still open. 14 of the 15 leads are marked open, not lost. Conversions often land days later, so this can recover without anyone acting.
